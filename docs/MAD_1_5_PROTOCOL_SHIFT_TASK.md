# MAD-1.5 protocol-shift task

Status: registered external-task development/replication plan; the first
96-system effect probe is development-only and no holdout claim is made.

## Why this is a different route

The existing 324 MatPES systems are closed to confirmatory relabeling. The
purpose of MAD-1.5 is therefore not to manufacture a MatPES holdout. It is a
new PBE-to-r2SCAN protocol-shift task in which the scientific question is
operational:

> Under a fixed number of expensive r2SCAN reveals, does a policy that sees
> only PBE observables find more target-protocol hull-supporting candidates,
> while preserving the complete revealed archive and reporting the cost of
> the policy?

This is a task-level method/mechanism result. It must not be described as an
independent MatPES generalization result, a paper-level holdout, or evidence
that IC-SARR is universally superior.

## Frozen task estimand

The unit is an exact chemical system. A system is eligible for the primary
mechanism table when it has at least 8 paired 3D-bulk candidates and at least
3 distinct stoichiometric compositions. The default query budget is

\[
B \in \{0,1,2,3,4,5,6\}.
\]

For each budget, report:

1. full-pool terminal target-hull confirmations;
2. final-causal confirmations among the selected trajectory;
3. the cumulative confirmation curve and its area under the curve;
4. wall-clock and phase-diagram construction cost;
5. paired system-level differences against source margin;
6. bootstrap intervals and win/tie/loss counts clustered by exact chemical
   system.

The target hull is composition-dependent and is recomputed from target
energies in the oracle vault. Querying a structure reveals its target energy;
the final-hull label is delayed and is never sent to the policy as a candidate
feature. All revealed target outcomes remain in the append-only archive.

## Policy and baseline contract

The first run compares only the already frozen policies:

- source margin;
- frozen IC-SARR / source-rollout implementation, once the MAD adapter has
  passed the existing action/reveal parity tests.

No MAD-specific score, posterior temperature, threshold, budget, or
composition fallback may be tuned after reading target outcomes. If an
adapter requires a new target-transport calibration, that calibration is a
separate development artifact and the policy checksum must be frozen before
the acquisition curve is read.

The policy-visible task contains PBE source observables, composition and
protocol fingerprints only. Target r2SCAN energies, target formation labels,
the complete oracle hull and unqueried target outcomes remain in the vault or
reveal boundary.

## Interpretation boundary

Positive evidence on this task would support only:

> Under the registered MAD-1.5 PBE-to-r2SCAN protocol-shift task and its
> fixed-budget oracle, the frozen policy has the reported acquisition-utility
> difference versus source margin.

It would not support an independent MatPES holdout claim, universal discovery
superiority, a memory/state-compression claim, or a causal-confirmation gain
unless those estimands separately pass their gates.

Negative evidence is still useful: it would show that the old development
signal does not transfer to this new protocol-shift task, without requiring a
new story about the 324 closed systems.

## Initial execution and optimization gate

The offline oracle-side nontriviality audit is
`E:\DATA\MAD-1.5-v1\mad15_hull_nontriviality_20260730.json`. Across all 1,884
systems, the mean number of target-hull stable candidates is 4.047, the mean
stable-candidate fraction is 0.330, and 306 systems are already saturated at
budget six. The task is therefore non-degenerate, but random high-budget
subsets can have little remaining headroom.

The source-margin curve on the same 50 outcome-independent hash-selected
systems gives oracle confirmations of 0.96, 1.90, 2.76, 3.30, 3.72 and 3.92
at budgets 1 through 6; the corresponding mean ceilings are 1.00, 1.96,
2.84, 3.44, 3.80 and 4.00. This is a baseline curve, not a superiority
result.

On the first 48-system fixed-backend IC-SARR probe at budget six and MC64,
source margin obtains 3.875 oracle confirmations/system and frozen IC-SARR
obtains 3.9375: 3 wins, 45 ties and 0 losses. Final-causal confirmation is
3.9583 versus 3.9792. A 24-system MC64/MC256 convergence check has identical
system traces and round actions at both integration levels, with 1 win and 23
ties. These are development mechanism probes only; no confidence interval or
paper claim is registered from them.

The already-parity-tested `fixed_composition` hull backend reduces IC-SARR
runtime on the 12-system MC64 probe from 14.412 to 4.502 seconds/system while
preserving all selected action IDs and oracle/final-causal metrics. Backend
identity remains part of the policy checksum, so implementation trace
checksums differ even when the legal action/reveal sequence is identical.

## 96-system frozen probe and interpretation

The next outcome-independent 96-system panel was evaluated with the same
fixed-composition backend and MC64 integration setting. The frozen
`independent_confirmation_source_rollout` policy versus `source_margin`
obtained an oracle-pool difference of `+0.0521 confirmations/system`, with
system-bootstrap 95% interval `[0.0000,+0.1042]`, Monte Carlo sign-flip
`p=0.1249`, and 6 wins, 89 ties and 1 loss. The final-causal difference was
`+0.0417/system`, with interval `[+0.0104,+0.0833]`, sign-flip `p=0.1246`,
and 4 wins, 92 ties and 0 losses. Mean wall time increased by
`+2.752 seconds/system`. The summary is reproducible from
`E:\DATA\MAD-1.5-v1\mad15_ic_probe_96_summary_20260730.json`; its input
runner SHA-256 is recorded there.

This result is compatible with a small acquisition signal, but the intervals
and sign-flip tests do not establish a confirmatory superiority claim. It is
development-only evidence for the registered MAD-1.5 task, not a MatPES
holdout, not a universal discovery result, and not evidence of a causal gain
or state-compression benefit. No further score, posterior, threshold or seed
tuning is authorized on this opened panel. The next useful step is a frozen
cross-fit or genuinely new protocol task, with the runtime cost included in
the prespecified comparison.
